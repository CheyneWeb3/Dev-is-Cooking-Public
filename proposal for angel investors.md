# Why Use NFT Shares

The NFT is not intended to operate like a speculative NFT collection.

It is simply a blockchain-based unit used to represent the ownership and economic allocation.

The system provides:

**1. Fixed share accounting**

1,000 units means ownership percentages are immediately understandable.

**2. Easy profit calculations**

The distribution system can read how many eligible NFTs each wallet holds and calculate that wallet's allocation.

**3. Transferability**

An investor could transfer an agreed investment interest to another eligible buyer without requiring the company to manually redesign its ownership calculations.

**4. Clear treasury allocations**

NFTs can be held in separate wallets representing founders, investors, treasury holdings, employee allocations, buybacks or future strategic investment.

**5. Separation from ecosystem tokens**

Investors are not required to buy or sell the company's project tokens.

This avoids tying company fundraising directly to token-market liquidity or token-price pressure.
