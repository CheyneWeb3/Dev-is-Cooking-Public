# Dev is Cooking — Angel Investment & Profit Share Proposal

## Overview

The proposal is to establish a simple **1,000-unit economic share structure** for the **Dev is Cooking ecosystem**, its existing operations, and future ventures developed and operated under the Cooking banner.

The investment is not based on one token, one application, or one development service.

Investors participate in the broader economic ecosystem being developed through **Dev is Cooking**, including current and future products, applications, infrastructure, services, commercial ventures, utilities, and other revenue-generating projects operating through the Cooking ecosystem.

As an additional benefit, a percentage of profits generated from commercial development work undertaken by the **Dev is Cooking Development Team** will also flow into the ecosystem profit treasury.

---

# 1. Share Unit Structure

The economic structure is divided into:

**1,000 total share units = 100%**

Therefore:

**1 share unit = 0.1%**

The proposed angel investment round makes available:

**125 share units = 12.5%**

for a total investment of:

**$40,000**

This establishes an initial round value of:

**$320 per share unit**

The existing ownership retains:

**875 share units = 87.5%**

# 6. Future Ventures Under the Cooking Banner

As new products and businesses are created, their applicable share of profits can contribute to the wider Dev is Cooking ecosystem.

Where Dev is Cooking owns 100% of a venture, its applicable profits can contribute to the Cooking profit treasury.

Where Dev is Cooking has only a percentage interest in another venture, only the portion belonging to Dev is Cooking contributes to the treasury.

For example:

A Cooking venture generates:

**$100,000 distributable profit**

Dev is Cooking owns or is entitled to:

**30%**

The amount attributable to the Cooking ecosystem is therefore:

**$30,000**

That $30,000 can then become part of the wider Dev is Cooking profit structure.

This allows the ecosystem's revenue base to expand as more successful ventures are created beneath the Cooking banner.

# 9. Development Profit Contribution

For qualifying commercial work performed through the Dev is Cooking Development Team:

**70% of applicable development profit remains with the development operation.**

**30% of applicable development profit flows into the Dev is Cooking Profit Treasury.**

The split applies to **profit after the direct costs of delivering the individual development project have been documented and accounted for.**

This means every development job can have its own transparent project accounting.

---

# 10. Fully Documented Development Costs

Each commercial development project should record:

**Contract value**

minus:

**Direct costs required to complete and deploy that particular project**

equals:

**Net development profit**

Direct project costs can include:

* servers;
* hosting;
* infrastructure;
* domains;
* paid APIs;
* third-party services;
* software licenses;
* deployment costs;
* external services;
* chain infrastructure;
* other costs directly attributable to delivering that project.

The resulting project profit is then allocated:

**70% — Development**

**30% — Dev is Cooking Profit Treasury**

This makes it clear what each development job earned, what it genuinely cost to deliver, and what was returned to the wider ecosystem.

**Work completed**

**30% Cooking treasury contribution**

# 15. Share Units and Transferability

Ownership is represented as **share units**.

The investor-facing structure remains simply:

**1,000 share units = 100%**

The blockchain/NFT technology behind the system is merely a technical mechanism for recording these units.

It can provide:

* verifiable ownership records;
* unit transfers;
* automated accounting;
* wallet-based ownership records;
* treasury allocations;
* distribution calculations;
* historical ownership records.

Investors do not need to think of themselves as purchasing an NFT.

They are purchasing an agreed number of **economic share units**.

The underlying blockchain representation is simply the infrastructure used to administer those units efficiently.

# 16. Transfer of Share Units

Subject to the company's final transfer rules and legal structure, share units can potentially be transferred between eligible parties.

For example:

An investor holds:

**30 units = 3%**

and transfers:

**10 units = 1%**

The original holder retains:

**20 units = 2%**

The incoming holder receives:

**10 units = 1%**

The underlying technical system can update the ownership record automatically.

This reduces the need to manually restructure the entire distribution calculation whenever ownership changes.
